04 The ritual

Three steps. Thirty seconds.

  1. 01

    Wet

    Rinse with warm — not hot — water. Hot water strips natural oils faster.

  2. 02

    Lather

    Two pumps into palms or a loofah. Massage in slow circles.

  3. 03

    Rinse & seal

    Rinse fully, pat dry, and moisturise while skin is still slightly damp.
